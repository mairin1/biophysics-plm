# Papers and code consulted

- METL-Global source dataset, Zenodo 14916528. https://zenodo.org/records/14916528

- EvoDiff, original model and implementation: https://github.com/microsoft/evodiff
- Li, Grandvalet, Davoine (2018), *Explicit Inductive Bias for Transfer Learning* (L2-SP). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/li18a.html
- ProteinGym, benchmark and evaluation protocols: https://github.com/OATML-Markslab/ProteinGym
- MIT 6.7960 Fall 2026, Lecture 18, *Post-training I: Transfer learning*, Phillip Isola (course material consulted when planning preservation strategies).

The papers motivate the experiments. They do not validate our particular implementation or the biological conclusions drawn from our results.
